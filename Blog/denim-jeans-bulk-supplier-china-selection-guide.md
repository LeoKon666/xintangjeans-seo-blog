## The Bulk Order Capacity Checklist

### 1. Production Line Verification
Ask for specifics: number of active production lines (you need minimum 3 for bulk orders), average daily output capacity (anything under 1,000 pairs per day is a red flag), and staffing levels per shift. If they can't show you 50+ sewing operators, they're not set up for serious volume.

### 2. Quality Control Systems
What we've seen repeatedly is that reliable bulk suppliers maintain dedicated QC teams positioned at multiple checkpoints throughout production. They'll have standardized measurement tolerance charts, not informal "we check everything" promises. Ask for photographic evidence of recent inspections — factories doing real quality control document their work.

### 3. Raw Material Sourcing
Confirm they have direct relationships with fabric mills, not trading companies acting as middlemen. A minimum 3-month fabric inventory ensures continuity when you need fast reorders. And testing certificates for restricted substances? Non-negotiable. To be fair, many smaller workshops skip this documentation entirely, which should immediately disqualify them from bulk consideration.
